OnDemand Andragogy
Kyriakos Tsoukalas [Colgate University]

Open OnDemand enables the execution of passenger applications, which can be utilized for self-paced learning and andragogy through GenAI-powered tools. The GenAI tutor paradigm can now be accessible on demand, within the same platform, to support a variety of user needs as soon as they arise. Open OnDemand passenger applications could enable guidance on basic coding, instructions for submitting jobs with different schedulers, "chatting" with available documentation, and accessing vital information via microservices such as resource availability, job-specific information, and efficiency reports.
